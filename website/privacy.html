Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 6, 2026

Summary

DesktopManager does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

DesktopManager collects no data. The app:

  • Does not connect to the internet
  • Does not collect analytics or telemetry
  • Does not track usage
  • Does not require an account

Local Configuration

All settings and rules you create are stored locally on your device in the application's local data folder. This data never leaves your computer.

GitHub Issues

If you choose to submit a bug report or feature request via GitHub Issues, you voluntarily provide that information. GitHub's privacy policy applies to any data you share there.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
